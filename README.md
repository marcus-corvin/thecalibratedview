# The Calibrated View

**See. Stay. Fill.**

A free nonfiction trilogy about perception, participation and leadership, by Marcus Corvin.

Read it online, one season to a page: <https://marcus-corvin.github.io/thecalibratedview/read/>
Or download the PDFs below. There is no sign-up and no email list.

## The three volumes

### Volume I: *The First Lens*. See What Is Actually There

The filters that your family, your culture, your history, the information you take in and your own body fitted before you could refuse them, and the observer who can learn to watch them work. The four seasons turn the lens on yourself, on other people, on the groups you belong to, and on the systems around all of them.

Spring: The 9 Lenses · Summer: The 9 Encounters · Fall: The 9 Patterns · Winter: The 9 Forces

[Read Volume I online](https://marcus-corvin.github.io/thecalibratedview/read/vol1/) · [Download the PDF](https://marcus-corvin.github.io/thecalibratedview/the-calibrated-view-vol1.pdf)

### Volume II: *The Full Room*. Stay Through What Matters

Crossing into the rooms where things that affect your life are decided, and staying once they turn uncomfortable: the first disagreement, the work nobody sees, and the weight of long membership.

Spring: The 8 Thresholds · Summer: The 8 Frictions · Fall: The 8 Weavings · Winter: The 8 Weights

[Read Volume II online](https://marcus-corvin.github.io/thecalibratedview/read/vol2/) · [Download the PDF](https://marcus-corvin.github.io/thecalibratedview/the-calibrated-view-vol2.pdf)

### Volume III: *The Gap*. Fill What Only You Can See

Fourteen people who noticed a gap nobody else was holding and stepped into it, followed through what woke them, the methods they built, what it cost them, and what remained after they stepped back.

Spring: The 7 Callings · Summer: The 7 Methods · Fall: The 7 Costs · Winter: The 7 Legacies

[Read Volume III online](https://marcus-corvin.github.io/thecalibratedview/read/vol3/) · [Download the PDF](https://marcus-corvin.github.io/thecalibratedview/the-calibrated-view-vol3.pdf)

Each volume stands on its own. Read them in order the first time.

## The author

Marcus Corvin is a pen name. The person behind it does not think that matters much.

He read about this for years before he tried any of it, and the trying went worse than the reading. That gap is where the trilogy came from.

He has watched the people who organise the carpool, hold the meeting together when everyone wants to leave, and carry the weight of something that would collapse without them without once calling it leadership. He has seen what it costs them.

He has also been the person who saw the gap and stayed seated. More often than he would like.

He does not believe in heroes. He believes in rooms, and in the ordinary people who fill them.
